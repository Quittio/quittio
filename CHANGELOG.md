# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Country packs for Austria (`quittio/country-at`), Belgium (`quittio/country-be`), Switzerland (`quittio/country-ch`) and France (`quittio/country-fr`), published as read-only split repositories next to `quittio/country-de` and not part of `quittio/quittio`. Their legal wording, rules and defaults are not reviewed by a tax adviser yet; see their READMEs.
- `DunningLaw::$b2cFixedRate` and `$b2bFixedRate`: fixed default interest rates for consumers and businesses instead of the reference rate plus points (e.g. 4 % for consumers in Austria, section 1000 (1) ABGB; 5 % in Switzerland, Art. 104 OR). `DefaultInterest` charges them for the whole time; `DefaultInterest::forCountry()` accepts a pack without reference rates when both rates are fixed (`DunningLaw::hasFixedRates()`).
- Payment slips: a payment QR code generator that implements `Pdf\Qr\ProvidesPaymentSlip` returns a `PaymentSlip`, which the default templates (plain PHP and Blade, partial `payment-slip`) print after the payment part of the Swiss QR bill: receipt and payment part at the bottom of a page of its own, the code at 46 × 46 mm without quiet zone. The view model has it as `payment.qrCode.slip`.
- Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License) is bundled as font family `liberationsans`, for the payment slip.
- `QrImage::svg()`, `png()` and `dataUri()` take `border: false` for a code without quiet zone.
- A regional locale such as `de_CH` takes the texts of its language (`de`) where it has none of its own, before English, in the core translator and the Laravel translator bridge; numbers and dates keep the regional format.
- Dutch labels, code names and payment terms texts (`nl`), e.g. for invoices from Flanders.

### Fixed

- `CustomTaxPreset::exemptionReason()` takes the text of the language of a regional locale (`fr` for `fr_BE`, `de` for `de_CH`) before the English one, so an exemption from `VatPreset::exempt()` of a country pack is printed in the language of an invoice with a regional locale.
- The mandatory field guard counts the register number (BT-30) as printed when the default templates show it in the footer, as it does for the tax identifiers.
- The Peppol rules treat the business process (BT-23) as the official rules do: an empty one is reported by `PEPPOL-EN16931-R001` and `-R007`, one of only whitespace by `-R007` and `-R008` instead of `-R001`.
- `PEPPOL-EN16931-R041` and `PEPPOL-EN16931-R042` report the path of the allowance or charge (`allowances[0].baseAmount`, `charges[0].percent`). Without it, an allowance and a charge with the same error were reported as one finding.
- CI ran every job on PHP 8.5, so PHP 8.3 and 8.4 were not tested; the jobs now use the PHP version they name, and PHPStan analyses for PHP 8.3 to 8.5.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-10-19

The legal wording, rules and defaults of the country pack Germany were reviewed against the UStG, the UStDV, the UStAE, the BMF letters of 15 October 2025 and 15 October 2026 and the case law, and confirmed by a tax adviser in October 2026. The changes below follow from that review. Quittio remains software, not tax advice; see the legal review and the disclaimer in the README.

### Added

- `DE-USTG-16` (warning): an invoice to a business, or for a service in connection with a property, issued more than six months after the supply (section 14 (2) sentence 2 UStG). Cancellations, corrections, prepayment invoices and re-issued invoices are skipped.
- `DE-USTG-17` (warning): a prepayment invoice (386) with an amount already paid (BT-113) but no VAT point date (BT-7). The date of receipt must be stated if it differs from the issue date (section 14 (4) sentence 1 no. 6 UStG).
- `->de()->serviceDateOpen()` for prepayment invoices whose date of the service is not agreed yet: flag `de.service_date_open` and the note `LegalTexts::SERVICE_DATE_OPEN` ("Der Zeitpunkt der Leistung ist noch nicht vereinbart."). `DE-USTG-03` no longer warns about the missing date then (section 14.8 (4) sentence 5 UStAE).
- `->de()->smallBusinessId('DE123456789EX')` for small businesses that state their small business identification number (suffix `EX`) instead of the tax number (section 34a sentence 1 no. 2 UStDV). It sets the number as tax number (BT-32) and the small business flag. `VatIdFormat::isSmallBusinessId()` recognises such numbers.
- `QUITTIO-SME-ID` (info): a small business identification number (suffix `EX`) in a VAT identifier field (BT-31, BT-48, BT-63). It is no VAT identifier (Article 284 (3) VAT Directive) and cannot be checked in VIES.
- `UstgRules::supplyDate()`: the supply date that `DE-USTG-08`, `DE-USTG-15` and `DE-USTG-16` use.
- Tax presets can require an invoice note: presets that implement `Quittio\Core\Tax\StatesInvoiceNote` give a text (`invoiceNote()`), which the builder adds once as note (BT-22, subject `REG`) when a line uses the preset, unless the invoice has a note with the same text. The PDF lists such a text once, also when it is the exemption reason (BT-120) as well.
- The German margin scheme, travel agency and triangular transaction presets state their text of section 14a (6) and (7) UStG also as invoice note, as the mapping table of KoSIT and FeRD assigns these statements to the invoice note (BG-1). `VatPreset::ZeroRatedPv` adds the note "Steuersatz 0 % nach § 12 Abs. 3 UStG." (`LegalTexts::ZERO_RATED_PV`), which category Z cannot carry as exemption reason.
- `VatPreset::BusinessTransfer` (`de.business_transfer`): transfer of a business as a whole (Geschäftsveräußerung im Ganzen, section 1 (1a) UStG), category O with `VATEX-EU-O` and the text "Nicht steuerbare Geschäftsveräußerung im Ganzen (§ 1 Abs. 1a UStG).". Like every category O invoice it states tax numbers instead of VAT identifiers (BR-O-02).
- `->de()->discountAgreement('der Bonusvereinbarung vom 01.01.2026')` refers to agreed bonuses or rebates whose amount is not known yet (section 14 (4) no. 7 UStG) with the note `LegalTexts::DISCOUNT_AGREEMENT`.
- `Quittio::correction($original, $number, reason: …)` (core and Laravel facade, `Corrections\Correction::of()`) starts a corrected invoice (384) that replaces the original: a copy with the new number, BG-3 and the note "Diese Rechnung berichtigt und ersetzt die Rechnung … vom …" (label `correction_note`), titled by the policy (`correction`, Germany: "Rechnungskorrektur"). Credit notes and self-billed documents are rejected. The new rule `DE-USTG-18` (warning) reports a 384 without such a note (section 31 (5) UStDV, section 15.2a (7) UStAE).
- `Quittio::priceReduction($original, $number, PriceReductionReason::CashDiscount, percent: '2')` (core and Laravel facade, `Corrections\PriceReduction::of()`) builds a document on the reduction of the consideration (section 17 UStG): a credit note (381) with one line per reduced VAT group, BG-3, the flag `price_reduction` and a note that names reason, net amount and VAT per rate. The reduction is a percentage, a net amount (one VAT group) or net amounts per VAT group (`'S:19' => '50.00'`). The German policy titles it "Entgeltminderung" (title `price_reduction`).
- `CorrectionPolicy` takes a `strategyResolver` that chooses the cancellation strategy per original (`cancellationStrategyFor()`); `Quittio::cancellation()` uses it when neither the argument nor the configuration sets one. The German pack cancels self-billed invoices with a Leitweg-ID (sent as XRechnung) by a negative self-billed invoice (389) instead of 261, which XRechnung does not list (`BR-DE-17`).
- Bonus statements (`DocumentType::BonusStatement`, `DocumentBuilder::bonusStatement()`, `Quittio::bonusStatement()` in Laravel): the reduction of the consideration of the supplies of a period, e.g. an annual bonus, per VAT rate (section 17 (4) UStG). `bonusPeriod()`, `bonusAgreement()` and `bonusInvoices()` set period, agreement and invoices (`DocumentData::$bonus`, `BonusStatement`); `bonus()` and `bonusOnRevenue()` add one reduction per VAT rate as a line. The PDF names period and agreement (label `bonus_statement_note`, with "§ 17 Abs. 4 UStG" from the German pack), names reductions instead of prices, VAT and amounts ("Minderung", "Minderung USt. 19 %", "Bonus gesamt (brutto)") and shows no bank details or amount due. Reductions for intra-community supplies and exports state "Minderung des Entgelts für steuerfreie innergemeinschaftliche Lieferungen" or "… Ausfuhrlieferungen" instead of the exemption text of a supply.
- The PDF prints the text of a special scheme once when it is also part of the joined exemption reason (BT-120) of a mixed exempt group; the note (BT-22) stays in the XML.
- Documents have plausibility notices (`DocumentData::notices()`, `RenderedDocument::notices()` in core and Laravel; the render run logs them as warnings): `QUITTIO-DUNNING-FLAT-RATE-FEES` for a reminder to a business that claims the flat rate and reminder fees, which count towards it (section 288 (5) sentence 3 BGB).
- `DE-USTG-19` (info): a final invoice deducts an advance settled by self-billing (389) but is no self-billed invoice itself (flag `de.self_billed_advance`, set by `deductAdvance()`).
- `->de()->advance($expectedService, receivedOn: …)` builds a prepayment invoice (386): the note "Anzahlungsrechnung. Die Leistung wird voraussichtlich am … / im … erbracht." with the expected date or month (`"2027-03"`), or without one the note that the date is not agreed yet. A date of receipt is stated as VAT point date (BT-7) and in the note "Die Anzahlung wurde am … vereinnahmt." (`LegalTexts::PREPAYMENT_MONTH`, `PREPAYMENT_RECEIVED`, flag `de.expected_service_date`).
- `->de()->deductAdvance($advance, received: …)` deducts only the amount received: a part is distributed over the VAT rows of the advance invoice and the VAT taken out of each part (section 14.8 (5) UStAE); `GermanInvoice::RECEIVED_IN_FULL` deducts the whole advance. Without `received:` the whole advance is deducted as before, and the new warning `DE-USTG-20` asks to confirm the amount (flag `de.advance_receipt_unconfirmed`).
- `DocumentBuilder::flatRate($claim, $amount = '40.00')` adds the flat rate for recovery costs of a late payment (Article 6 Directive 2011/7/EU, section 288 (5) BGB) for one claim, as `DunningFee` of the new kind `DunningFeeKind::FlatRate` with the claim (`DunningFee::$kind`, `$claim`). Building a reminder rejects a second flat rate of the same claim and a flat rate for a consumer.
- Country packs can complete an invoice before it is calculated, validated and rendered (`Countries\Capabilities\CompletesInvoices::complete()`), with their settings from `Config::$countrySettings` (core: `countries.{code}`, Laravel: `quittio.countries.{code}`).
- The German pack leaves out the VAT identifiers of seller and buyer on invoices of category O only (not subject to VAT), which EN 16931 forbids (BR-O-02), when the seller's tax number is given and there is no tax representative: flag `de.vat_ids_omitted`, info `DE-CATEGORY-O-01`. Otherwise the info `DE-CATEGORY-O-02` names what is missing. Setting `quittio.countries.de.omit_vat_ids_for_category_o` (default `true`).
- The German pack adds the default notice of section 286 (3) BGB (`LegalTexts::CONSUMER_DEFAULT`, subject `PMT`) to every invoice for a consumer that does not have one: without it the 30 days do not apply to consumers. Switch it off with `quittio.countries.de.consumer_default_notice = false`.
- The German pack checks the e-invoice obligation of section 14 (1) and section 27 (38) UStG when a run produces no e-invoice (plain PDF, MINIMUM, BASIC WL): `DE-EINVOICE-01` (error) when an e-invoice is required (supplies from 2028, of 2027 with a prior-year turnover above 800,000 EUR, or after the end of the transition), `DE-EINVOICE-02` (warning) while the transition still allows an other invoice, as PDF only with the buyer's consent. Not for consumers, buyers or sellers outside the Inland, small amount invoices up to 250 EUR, small businesses, tickets (`->de()->ticket()`), supplies exempt under section 4 no. 8 to 29 UStG only, cancellations and corrections, nor for drafts. Prepayment invoices count with their issue date. New setting `quittio.countries.de.prior_year_turnover_above_800k` (default `false`). Both findings are BMF format errors.
- A PDF or HTML rendered in one run with an XML-only e-invoice (e.g. `['xrechnung-cii', 'pdf']`) states in its legal notes that it is a readable copy: "Lesbare Darstellung der E-Rechnung (XRechnung 3.0) Nr. … – inhaltsgleiches Mehrstück. Maßgeblich ist ausschließlich die XML-Datei." (label `einvoice_visualization`). The render option `visualization_notice` = `false` leaves it out.
- `tools/validate-fixtures.php` writes 13 further German documents (small business with identification number, prepayment invoice, final invoices with full, partial and remainder deduction, self-billed invoice and both of its cancellations, corrected invoice, price reduction, business transfer, photovoltaic, margin scheme) in EN 16931 and XRechnung, CII and UBL, and as ZUGFeRD PDF, so that KoSIT and veraPDF check them in CI.
- The validation context carries whether the run produces an e-invoice (option `e_invoice`) and the settings of the country packs (option `countries`).
- `->de()->deductAdvance($advance, style: GermanInvoice::REMAINDER)` deducts the advance as negative lines per VAT rate instead of the prepaid amount (remainder invoice, section 14.8 (11) UStAE), with the note `LegalTexts::ADVANCE_DEDUCTION_REMAINDER`. The default `PREPAID` stays (BT-113 with the itemisation in BT-22, accepted by the BMF letter of 15 October 2026 until 30 June 2030).

### Changed

- `DE-USTG-06` (missing "Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers") is an error instead of a warning: the statement is mandatory (section 14a (1) sentence 1 and (5) sentence 1 UStG). The buyer owes the VAT all the same.
- `DE-USTG-11` no longer requires a VAT identifier of a small business seller for reverse charge services in another member state and triangular transactions (section 19 (1) sentence 2 UStG). The buyer's VAT identifier is still required.
- Small business invoices (section 34a UStDV) are recognised by the small business flag as well: `UstgRules::isSmallBusinessInvoice()` is true when the flag is set or a line uses `VatPreset::SmallBusiness` (before: every line). `DE-USTG-03` and the mandatory PDF output skip the service date for them, as `DE-USTG-09` and `DE-USTG-14` already did.
- `DE-USTG-15` checks final invoices (BG-3 with a prepaid amount BT-113): on intra-community supplies advance payments trigger no VAT, so the final invoice has the deadline. Re-issued invoices with BG-3 and no prepaid amount are still skipped.
- `DE-USTG-08` takes the small amount limit of the supply date instead of the issue date (the raise to 250 EUR applies to supplies from 1 January 2017), and the issue date only without a supply date.
- `DE-USTG-04` (missing "Gutschrift") is a warning when the XML is written: in an e-invoice the type code labels a self-billed invoice (section 14.5 (24) sentence 1 UStAE). It stays an error otherwise.
- `QUITTIO-VATID-FORMAT` no longer reports small business identification numbers, and `DE-TAX-NUMBER` accepts them as tax number of a small business.
- German default interest counts actual/actual: `CountryDE::dunningLaw()->dayCount` is `null` instead of 365, so the days of a leap year are divided by 366 (as the statutory conventions of the annex to section 16 PAngV do); a full leap year yields exactly the annual rate. `DunningLaw::$dayCount` accepts `null`.
- `LegalTexts::PREPAYMENT` reads "Anzahlungsrechnung. Die Leistung wird voraussichtlich am :date erbracht." ("Advance invoice. The service is expected to be performed on :date."): the sentence that VAT becomes due on receipt is not required and was dropped.
- The PDF and HTML recognise margin scheme and travel agency lines by their own exemption code: their lines show no rate also when the exempt VAT group lost its code because other exemptions share it (`QUITTIO-EXEMPTION-CONFLICT`), and a group of different special schemes is shown as special scheme. An exempt group that mixes a special scheme with other exemptions is shown in two rows of `vatRows` ("Sonderregelung, kein gesonderter Steuerausweis: …" and "Umsatzsteuerfrei … auf …"), or in one row without rate and VAT amount (`vat_row_exempt_special_scheme`) when an exempt document level allowance or charge makes the parts unknown. `ViewModelFactory::mixedExemptGroup()` and `vatRowIndexes()` tell templates and country packs which row shows which VAT breakdown row.
- The readable copy notice of a PDF next to an XRechnung is the last of the `legalNotes`, so that the note paths of `MandatoryFieldGuard::notePath()` stay valid.
- The text of `VatPreset::NotTaxableInGermany` names the place of supply abroad: "Nicht im Inland steuerbare Leistung (Leistungsort im Ausland)" ("Service not taxable in Germany (place of supply abroad)").
- The note of a partial cancellation whose VAT is aligned with the earlier ones (`cancellation_vat_aligned`) explains the rounding: "Rundungsausgleich: Die Umsatzsteuer dieses Teilstornos ist so bemessen, dass alle Teilstornos zur Rechnung RE-… zusammen deren Umsatzsteuer exakt berichtigen." The label takes the placeholder `:number`; overridden labels without it keep working.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- `MimeType::Xml` (`application/xml`) for supporting documents (BT-125-1) in the profile `xrechnung-extension`, which allows XML attachments (BR-DEX-01). The PHP rules now check the MIME code with `BR-CL-24`: other profiles report `MimeType::Xml` as error, as the official Schematron does (`BR-CL-24`, in Peppol also `PEPPOL-EN16931-CL001`); the extension accepts it through `ExtendsCodeLists::additionalCodes()`.

### Changed

- The VAT rows of the PDF and HTML name IGIC (category L, Canary Islands) and IPSI (category M, Ceuta and Melilla) instead of VAT, e.g. "7 % IGIC auf 50,00 €" or "7% IGIC on 50.00 €" (labels `vat_row_igic` and `vat_row_ipsi`). Rows of the other categories are unchanged.
- The package rules `QUITTIO-DECLARED-AMOUNTS`, `QUITTIO-VAT-ROUNDING` and `QUITTIO-CANCELLATION-VAT-ALIGNED` read the declared amounts state through the internal `Calculation\DeclaredAmountsState` like the calculator, the builder, the reader and the cancellations. The results are the same.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Typed view model: every node that templates receive has a class in `Quittio\Core\View\Model` (`PartyView`, `AddressView`, `LineView`, `VatRowView`, `TotalsView`, `PaymentView`, `DesignView`, `DocumentInfoView` …; lists are `ViewList<T>`, e.g. `ViewList<LineView>`) with its keys as typed properties, so IDEs and PHPStan know that `$invoice->totals->amountDue` is a `?FormattedValue`. The nodes are `ViewNode` objects over the same data: published Blade and PHP templates, array syntax, `toArray()` and the mandatory field guard work unchanged ([Typed nodes](docs/templates.md#typed-nodes)). The compatibility promise now names the keys and types of the view model.
- The PHP rules check the XRechnung extension like the official validator: `BR-DEX-02` (warning), `BR-DEX-04` to `BR-DEX-10` and `BR-DEX-12` to `BR-DEX-14` for identifier schemes with `XR01` to `XR03`, the amount due with third party payments and the third party payments themselves. In the profile `xrechnung-extension` they replace `BR-CL-10`, `-11`, `-21`, `-25`, `-26` and, in UBL, `BR-CO-16` and `QUITTIO-SUBLINES`, so a violation is reported once. Profiles declare such replacements with the capability `ReplacesRules`.
- The Peppol profile checks all Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 rules that the data of an invoice can break, with readable messages instead of only the official validation: the format of the business process (`PEPPOL-EN16931-R007`), values of only whitespace (`-R008`), net price against gross price and discount (`-R046`), the sign of BT-111 (`-R055`), line periods within the invoicing period (`-R110`, `-R111`), currencies and dates (`-CL007`, `-F001`), the type codes of business process 01 (`-P0100`, `-P0101`), the type codes 326 and 384 outside Germany (`-P0112`), VATEX codes with the wrong VAT category (`-P0104` to `-P0111`) and the format and check digits of GLN, Norwegian, Danish, Belgian, Italian, Swedish and Australian identifiers (`PEPPOL-COMMON-R040` to `-R053`). The code lists `CodeList::PEPPOL_CURRENCIES`, `PEPPOL_INVOICE_TYPES` and `PEPPOL_CREDIT_NOTE_TYPES` are generated from the Peppol rules with `CodeListGenerator::generatePeppol()` (`CodeListGenerator::PEPPOL_LISTS`). The rules the UBL writer always satisfies are listed in the validation docs (Peppol rules).
- The PHP validation checks the VAT categories L (IGIC, Canary Islands: `BR-AF-01`, `-02`, `-05`, `-08`, `-09`, `-10`), M (IPSI, Ceuta and Melilla: `BR-AG-01`, `-02`, `-05`, `-08`, `-09`, `-10`) and B (split payment, Italy: `BR-B-01`, `BR-B-02`). Like S, L and M allow one VAT breakdown per rate with VAT; their rate may also be 0. The warning `QUITTIO-IGIC-ZERO-RATE-CII` reports IGIC with the rate 0 in CII, which the official CII Schematron rejects.
- XRechnung CVD (Clean Vehicles Directive) in the German pack: the profile `xrechnung-cvd` (`XRechnung30Cvd`, specification identifier `…#compliant#urn:xeinkauf.de:kosit:xrechnung:cvd_0.9`) for `xrechnung-cii`, `xrechnung-ubl` and `zugferd`, with the rules `BR-DE-CVD-01` to `BR-DE-CVD-06-b`: contract (BT-12) and tender or lot reference (BT-17) are required, and vehicle lines carry the category as item classification with scheme `CVD` (BT-158) and the item attribute `cva` (BT-160, BT-161). The profile accepts the scheme `CVD` in the code list check `BR-CL-13`.

### Changed

- The Laravel settings of the German pack live under `quittio.countries.de` (`config('quittio.countries.de.base_rates')`, `….base_rate_file`) instead of the configuration `quittio-de`. `php artisan vendor:publish --tag=quittio-de-config` publishes them to `config/quittio/countries/de.php`, which Laravel loads as `quittio.countries.de`; they can also be set in `config/quittio.php` under `'countries' => ['de' => […]]`. `quittio:install` publishes the tag `quittio-{code}-config` of every installed country pack with `config/quittio.php`. `GermanyServiceProvider` no longer fills `quittio-de` with defaults: code that reads `config('quittio-de.…')` reads `quittio.countries.de`. Lower-case codes under `quittio.countries` hold the settings of a pack, the convention for further packs (`quittio.countries.{code}`); a pack class listed under `'de'` is moved to a numeric key.
- The calculator, the builder, the reader and the cancellations read and write the declared amounts flags together as one typed state (the internal `Calculation\DeclaredAmountsState`) instead of single flags. The flags stay the stored format with the same strings, so stored data of earlier versions is read unchanged; they are now always written after the other flags in a fixed order (`declared_amounts`, `declared_amounts_recalculated`, `cancellation_vat_aligned`, `declared_vat_in_tax_currency:`, `amounts_fingerprint:` last), and duplicates or an unreadable `declared_vat_in_tax_currency:` value are dropped when data is calculated again.
- `ViewModelFactory` builds the view model with one internal factory per section (header, parties, references, lines, allowances and charges, VAT breakdown, totals, payment, notes, design, documents; `View\Internal\Sections`). The view model is the same as before.

### Deprecated

- The configuration `quittio-de` (`config/quittio-de.php`): move its keys to `quittio.countries.de` and delete the file. Until 1.0 it is still read: its keys fill those that `quittio.countries.de` leaves out or keeps at the default, and the provider reports an `E_USER_DEPRECATED` on every start while it is set ([The old file](docs/configuration.md#the-old-file-configquittio-dephp)).

### Fixed

- The KoSIT validator (L5) reports a rule with the level the validator scenario gives it (`customLevel`), as the validator itself does when it decides on acceptance. Before, the level of the Schematron was used, so e.g. BR-CO-16 in an XRechnung extension invoice with third party payments was reported as error although the validator accepted the document.
- The official validation in CI now checks PDF/A-3b with veraPDF: the job pulls the `verapdf/cli` image (pinned by digest), and the veraPDF tests fail instead of being skipped when the image is missing in CI. `tools/validate-fixtures.sh` also renders every EN 16931 scenario as ZUGFeRD PDF and checks the PDFs with veraPDF; before, it generated no PDFs, and its veraPDF call would have received no files.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added

- Single PHP rules can be switched off, re-rated or replaced by their code: `validation.rules.disabled`, `validation.rules.severity` and `Quittio::extend('rule', …)` in Laravel, `RuleOverrides` with `Quittio::configureRules()` in the core. Official EN 16931 and Peppol rules need `allow_official`; unknown codes are refused. `Quittio::ruleCodes()` lists the known codes.
- The calculation policy decides what happens with declared amounts of other systems: `calculation.declared_amounts` (`keep`, `recalculate`, `reject`), `CalculationPolicy` and `DeclaredAmountsMode` in the core. An own calculator is configured with `calculation.calculator` or passed to `Quittio::create(calculator: …)`; `Quittio::calculator()` returns it.
- `Quittio::edit()` (core and Laravel) and `InvoiceBuilder::fromUsing()` continue existing invoice data with the calculator of the instance; cancellations and the document calculator of `QuittioFake` use it as well. `InvoiceFactory::calculated()` takes a calculator.
- Tax presets whose rate depends on the date (`Quittio\Core\Tax\DatedTaxPreset`) are resolved with the supply date of each line (`SupplyDate`: line period, VAT point date, delivery date, invoicing period, issue date). `DatedValues` holds values that change on dates; `TaxPresetCollection::alias()` finds presets under further keys.
- `VatPreset::Standard` (`de.standard`) and `VatPreset::Reduced` (`de.reduced`) take the German rate of the supply date (`TaxTables`), e.g. 16 % and 5 % from July to December 2020. The plausibility rule `DE-VAT-RATE` warns about rates that applied neither as standard nor as reduced rate on the supply date. The small amount limit of `DE-USTG-08` is that of the issue date (150.00 EUR before 2017).
- Country packs register output formats, aliases and serializers (`Countries\Capabilities\ProvidesFormats`, implemented by `AbstractCountryPack`); the German pack brings XRechnung (`XRechnungFormats`). Profiles declare sub lines, extension fields and third party payments with capabilities (`SupportsSubLines`, `SupportsExtensionFields`, `SupportsThirdPartyPayments`) instead of the core checking XRechnung keys.
- Default interest for every country pack with a reference rate: `Quittio\Core\Dunning\DefaultInterest` (`forCountry($pack)`), the contract `Dunning\ReferenceRates` and the capability `ProvidesReferenceRates`; the German pack provides its base rates (`BaseRateTable` implements `ReferenceRates`) and its `DefaultInterest` uses the core calculation. `DocumentBuilder::interestCharge()` adds calculated charges with their day count and texts.
- Documents can be queued in Laravel: `Quittio::document($quote)->queue()->save($disk, $path)` with the job `RenderDocument`, the events `QueuedDocumentStored` and `QueuedDocumentFailed` and `QuittioManager::queueDocument()`; `QuittioFake` records them (`assertDocumentQueued()`, `queuedDocuments()`). Documents are stored as versioned snapshots (`DocumentData::toSnapshot()`, `fromSnapshot()`, `toArray()`, `toJson()` …) with their own upcasters (`quittio.snapshots.document_upcasters`, `SnapshotUpcasterChain::documentDefault()`); `Mapping\Attributes\MapOf` keeps keyed arrays.
- The German special cases are reached with the builder macro `de()`: `->de()->smallAmount()`, `->de()->selfBilled()`, `->de()->deductAdvance($advance)` … (`GermanInvoice`).

- `quittio:validate --kosit/--verapdf` uses every driver setting (`java`, `docker`, `timeout`, `fail_on_unavailable`) and no longer changes the cached core; `defaults.format = 'auto'` is accepted in Laravel and as issuer default; `quittio:preview` and the preview route accept the views of `pdf.templates`; the facade docblock lists every manager and fake method; the ASCII fallback file name of downloads transliterates characters ("Ü" becomes "U").
- Profile validation no longer warns about postal addresses in BASIC WL and BASIC; address fields carry their Factur-X levels and the business terms of their context (e.g. BT-55 for the buyer country). Data of EXTENDED and of the XRechnung extension, and fields below a field that is not allowed, are reported once.
- Reading a CII or UBL invoice without document totals reports BR-12 to BR-15 instead of `QUITTIO-NOT-CALCULATED`.
- In the view model of documents (quotes, reminders …) `document.typeCode` is `null` and `document.type` names the document type; the default templates highlight lines marked with `highlight()` and use `colors.secondary`; `CountryDefaults::$paymentQr` selects the QR code of the mode `auto`; self-billed invoices take their title from the country pack's correction policy.
- `paymentTerms()` with a callable continues existing terms, also after `InvoiceBuilder::from()`.
- Country pack Germany: `DE-USTG-06` accepts the pack's reverse charge texts in every shipped language; `newVehicle()` adds the note of section 14a (4) UStG; `deductAdvance()` writes the whole note in one language and rejects documents that cannot be advance invoices (credit notes, corrections, negative totals); `VatPreset::exempt()` defaults to German like the enum presets; `DefaultInterest` uses the day count of the dunning law (365) and `BaseRateTable::periods()` merges periods with the same rate.
- Floats as amounts, prices, rates and quantities are rejected with an `InvalidArgumentException` also in files without `declare(strict_types=1)`; before, PHP turned `netPrice(19.99)` into `19` for parameters typed `BigDecimal|int|string`. The same applies to `InvoiceLine::withNetPrice()`/`withPriceInclVat()`, `CustomTaxPreset::make()` and partial cancellations of the Managed layer.
- UBL credit notes write the tender or lot reference (BT-17) as `OriginatorDocumentReference` in the element order of `CreditNote`; it was dropped before.
- Default line identifiers skip identifiers set with `id()` (a line without `id()` and one with `id('1')` both got `1`); identifiers used twice make `build()` throw `InvalidInvoiceData`, and data from other sources gets the warning `QUITTIO-LINE-ID-DUPLICATE`.
- The configured profile (`defaults.profile`) applies to every format that allows it, also to `->format('zugferd')` and formats chosen by a country rule; `Config::$profile` defaults to `null` (format or automatic choice decides). XRechnung CII with sub lines reports the sub lines in the validation instead of failing with "does not support profile extended".
- The `template` of the presentation passed to `->presentation()` and of the configured presentation selects the template (after `->template()` and the invoice's own presentation).
- Laravel: country packs listed in `quittio.countries` replace discovered packs with the same code (e.g. a subclass of `CountryDE`).
- `swiss-qr-bill` without a generator fails with a `ConfigurationException` instead of printing no code; generators are registered with `Quittio::withPaymentQrGenerator()` or `quittio.pdf.payment_qr.generator`. PDF renderers whose `supportsEmbedding()` is `false` get PDF/A and the embedded XML from `MpdfPostProcessor`; `CallbackRenderer` and `PassthroughRenderer` report `true`.
- A missing `java`, `verapdf` or `docker` binary is reported as `KOSIT-UNAVAILABLE`/`VERAPDF-UNAVAILABLE` instead of aborting the render run with an `ErrorException` in Laravel.
- Country pack Germany: invoices with `VatPreset::ZeroRatedPv` (section 12 (3) UStG) failed BR-Z-10, because the preset sent an exemption reason; the zero rate is now stated as the rate only.
- Country pack Germany: order numbers shaped like a Leitweg-ID (e.g. `4711-01`) no longer switch the format to XRechnung; only a Leitweg-ID with valid check digits or a `0204` endpoint does, and the check digit warning is limited to public buyers.
- Country pack Germany: "Gutschrift" is required for all self-billed type codes (261, 389, 527), not only 389.
- Country pack Germany: default interest texts named the first day of the next period as end date; they now name the last day with interest.
- Country pack Germany: the small amount simplifications (section 33 UStDV) no longer apply to reverse charge, intra-community supplies and distance sales (section 33 sentence 3 UStDV, rule `DE-USTG-12`); such invoices need all mandatory information and are not rendered as plain PDF.
- Country pack Germany: `DE-USTG-09` also rejects VAT on document level charges of small businesses; `DE-USTG-01` requires the postcode of German buyers; `DE-USTG-11` also applies to reverse charge lines without a preset when the buyer is in another member state.
- Country pack Germany: `->deductAdvance()` rejects advance invoices in another currency and deducts the amount received (including a cash rounding BT-114).
- Country pack Germany: the base rate table rejects dates from the next scheduled change (1 January or 1 July) after the last known rate instead of silently using the old rate (`BaseRateTable::validUntil()`); the § 288 (5) BGB flat rate uses the money context of the documents.
- `quittio.storage.disk` is used as the default disk of `save()`.
- Package dependencies: `quittio/core` requires `ext-gd` (needed by mPDF) and `ext-ctype`, `quittio/country-de` declares Brick, `quittio/laravel` and `quittio/managed` require `laravel/framework` (they use Illuminate\Foundation); the autoload prefix of `quittio/laravel` is `Quittio\Laravel\`.
- Calculation: exemption reasons are only written into VAT breakdowns of categories that allow them (E, AE, K, G, O); S and Z breakdowns no longer fail BR-S-10/BR-Z-10.
- Calculation: gross prices and document allowances are distributed with signed weights, so vouchers, returns and negative lines keep their sign and correct net amounts; a negative gross price is rejected.
- Calculation: document allowances and charges accept an exemption reason and code for VAT groups they create alone; sub lines with a VAT category or rate other than their parent are rejected (`QUITTIO-SUBLINE-VAT`).
- XML: BT-6 is omitted when it equals the invoice currency (BR-53 in CII); MINIMUM writes only the seller country; a warning reports preceding invoices (BG-3) that CII cannot carry (`QUITTIO-PRECEDING-INVOICES-CII`); XRechnung BT-20 contains free text, due days and late fee terms before the `#SKONTO#` lines.
- Reading: exemption reasons of the VAT breakdown are copied to the lines, a declared BT-111 survives recalculation, all VATEX codes of the code list are accepted.
- PDF: watermarked drafts and previews never embed e-invoice XML (an embedded format with a watermark throws), watermarked documents and HTML show the watermark.
- PDF: the mandatory field guard only counts printed values (not `count()` or loops) and checks regulatory notes in `legalNotes`; the German pack uses the keys `servicePeriod`, line periods and `taxRepresentative.vatId`.
- PDF: the seller's tax representative (BG-11), payee (BG-10), delivery address (BG-13) and price base quantity (BT-149) are printed; every cash discount tier shows its own amount; credit notes show "Erstattungsbetrag" without bank details; titles follow the type code family (self-billed → "Gutschrift", credit notes → "Rechnungskorrektur", 325 → "Proforma-Rechnung", 386 → "Anzahlungsrechnung"); commercial credit notes are no longer labelled "Gutschrift" in the code lists.
- PDF: the DIN 5008 address field keeps its position with a logo; VAT rows are labelled per category; split allowances name their VAT rate; quotes and order confirmations show no payment block or GiroCode; the EPC payload respects the 331 byte limit; the render locale wins over the invoice locale.
- Security: template names from requests or options are never file paths (`PhpTemplateEngine` resolves names only inside the configured template directories); the preview route accepts only the default, the configured and the package templates.
- PDF/A: logos are re-encoded as RGB PNG on white (logos with transparency or above 600×200 px made every ZUGFeRD render fail), SVG logos are rasterised (Imagick) or rejected; the GiroCode PNG is compressed.
- PDF: the mandatory field guard checks every line (name, quantity), every VAT row, every legal note, the cash discount terms, both buyer name and address, the service date (BT-72/BG-14, otherwise every line period) and the full tax representative; null values no longer count as printed.
- PDF: self-billed invoices show the buyer as issuer and the seller as recipient, with payout wording and without GiroCode; margin scheme and travel agency rows state no VAT amount (section 14a (6) UStG); descriptions and notes are plain text by default (Markdown opt-in, it may change the displayed text); a watermark together with any XML format throws; per-call presentations merge over the configured one; payment reminders encode the total and the open invoice numbers in the GiroCode; DIN 5008: the recipient starts in the Anschriftzone.
- XMP: `fx:Version` is the XRechnung version for the XRECHNUNG conformance level, the XRechnung extension declares `XRECHNUNG`.
- XML (EXTENDED): exemption reasons on line and allowance/charge level, one payment terms group per cash discount tier, the exchange rate; German BT-20 free text uses German number and date formats.
- Builder: `payment()` replaces the payment instructions instead of accumulating accounts (duplicate IBAN and lost BT-83 in foreign currency invoices, BR-DE-25-b on direct debit); percentage allowances and charges follow their base after line changes; `InvoiceLine::withPriceInclVat()`, price modes cannot be mixed.
- Cancellations: read foreign currency invoices keep BT-111 (implied exchange rate), XRechnung third-party payments are reversed in full cancellations only, gross-price lines can be cancelled by amount, self-billed documents stay self-billed (389 ↔ 261).
- Laravel: `seller.identifiers` (BT-29) for sellers without VAT identifier; explicit `pdf.paper` wins over the country default; unknown letter layouts and QR types throw; queued renders travel as snapshots through the upcaster chain; removed the unused `defaults.currency`, `defaults.timezone` and `defaults.standard`.
- Reading: own PDF parser with cross-reference streams, object streams and incremental updates (PDFs from PDFBox, iText, Acrobat or Word could not be read); name tree loops, exponent numbers, oversized tags and attribute floods are rejected; strict dates (never replaced by today, BR-03 for a missing issue date); no default currency (BR-05), BT-110 only in the invoice currency, BT-149 from the gross price, BR-CL-06 for BT-8, BR-DEC as errors, sanitised attachment names, embedded files chosen via /AF with findings for ambiguous PDFs, a specific message for ZUGFeRD 1.0; MINIMUM and BASIC WL documents without lines no longer trigger line based rules.
- Reading API: `read()` and `validateFile()` take the content only, never a path; `readFile()` and `validatePath()` read files.
- Documents: proforma invoices (325) are rejected in the invoice pipeline (`QUITTIO-PROFORMA`, use `DocumentBuilder::proforma()`); payment reminders and account statements take no lines, the reminder total is open items + fees + interest without VAT and the GiroCode uses it with the open invoice numbers; account statements respect their period; interest uses actual/actual per calendar year and the text names the last interest day; level 1 reminders are titled "1. Mahnung".
- Countries: the buyer's country pack also applies for sellers outside the EU (e.g. XRechnung for German public buyers); `VatTerritory` knows the EU VAT territory (XI for goods, Monaco, Isle of Man, Büsingen, Helgoland and other excluded areas); VIES is only queried for EU member states and XI, and unavailable answers are not treated as invalid; L7 warnings for intra-community supplies and exports to the wrong destination.
- Partial cancellations reverse taxable amounts and VAT exactly: per VAT group the VAT of a cancellation is round2(cumulative cancelled taxable amount × rate) minus the VAT already cancelled, the closing cancellation takes the original's VAT; in gross price mode the net amounts are aligned the same way (cumulative gross / (1 + rate)), so BT-116 and BT-117 per group and BT-109 to BT-115 add up exactly over every sequence; each document stays within one cent of its own rounding and keeps the sign of its taxable amount (BR-CO-17); the same for BT-111 in foreign currency invoices; originals of other systems with their own rounding are aligned with their effective rate. Managed stores the cancelled amounts per group (`vat` on invoice relations), recomputes them under lock and refuses a cancellation that raced with another one; core users can pass a `CancellationHistory`, which is validated against the original. Aligned documents carry the info `QUITTIO-CANCELLATION-VAT-ALIGNED` and a note sentence when a group's VAT differs from its own rounding; drafts keep a declared VAT breakdown only for aligned cancellations.
- Reading PDFs: the reconstruction of damaged files scans one object at a time and stops above 100,000 objects, PNG predictors only process the data present, the catalogue search no longer caches every object (memory and CPU exhaustion with small crafted files).
- PDF: invoices with line groups (`->group()`) failed to render under Laravel (mPDF warning with `packTableData`); refunds and self-billed invoices no longer announce a direct debit; SVG logos are read only with the SVG coder at a bounded resolution, logos above 10 MB or 25 megapixels are rejected; the GiroCode beneficiary falls back to the seller name; partials cannot be previewed as templates.
- Reading XML: encodings that hide the markup from the pre-scan (UTF-7, EBCDIC, UTF-32) and DOCTYPEs are rejected before libxml parses them, namespace declarations in scope are limited (CPU exhaustion with libxml < 2.12); BT-6 is no longer interpolated into XPath (injection of BT-111); relative date strings ("tomorrow 2026", "+2000 days") are rejected also in the builder; cash discount periods in weeks; bidi control characters are stripped from attachment names.
- Calculation: different exemption reasons in one VAT group are joined in BT-120 (e.g. § 4 Nr. 14 UStG together with a margin scheme), BT-121 is only kept when the codes agree (`QUITTIO-EXEMPTION-CONFLICT` warning); percentage document allowances follow removed lines; line ids no longer collide after `removeLine()` and `line()`.
- Cancellations of self-billed invoices are titled "Storno-Gutschrift" with the note "Stornierung der Gutschrift …" (`CorrectionPolicy::cancellationTitle()`, title key `cancellation_self_billed`).
- Country pack Germany: the § 3c distance sale exclusion of small amount invoices uses the VAT territory and the deliver-to address and only warns; "Gutschrift" may be given in another official EU language (UStAE 14.5 Abs. 24); small amount invoices in a foreign currency are compared by their EUR equivalent.
- PDF: remote logos are fetched by Quittio only from public addresses (checked per resolved IP and redirect, connected to the checked IP), mPDF never fetches URLs; Blade templates are restricted to an allowlist (`quittio.pdf.templates`, default `invoices.*`); the guard requires the unit and the seller's legal name; refunds of negative invoices are shown as positive amounts; `Presentation::$explicit` allows per-invoice resets.
- Cancellations and unchanged `InvoiceBuilder::from()` keep the declared line and VAT amounts of read invoices within the EN 16931 rounding tolerance, so foreign invoices using other rounding are reversed exactly (`QUITTIO-DECLARED-AMOUNTS` warns when amounts had to be recalculated); an exchange rate implied from BT-111 is dropped once amounts change; amount discounts split across VAT rates are re-split after line changes; third-party payments (BG-DEX-09) are rejected where the syntax cannot carry them (`QUITTIO-THIRD-PARTY-PAYMENTS`).
- Validation knows the XML syntax that is written, so syntax specific rules apply (e.g. BR-DEX-15 for XRechnung CII); UBL writes BT-82 and the credit note due date only once with several accounts (UBL-SR-45/46).
- PDF: mPDF cannot fetch anything over HTTP (deny-all client), logo values are strictly classified (no backslashes, protocol-relative URLs or surrounding whitespace; data URIs strictly decoded and re-encoded; SVG entity declarations refused in every mode); IPv6 site-local addresses are refused for remote logos.
- Declared amounts: only set when the recalculation does not reproduce them, line net amounts kept only within 0.02 of the unrounded formula (`QUITTIO-LINE-ROUNDING`), and only while a fingerprint of the amount inputs matches (also for read data edited before `from()`), so edits outside the builder never keep stale amounts; categories without VAT never keep a declared VAT amount; PEPPOL-EN16931-R120 uses the official tolerance of 0.02.
- Country pack Germany: `DE-XRECHNUNG-EXT-01` rejects credit notes of the XRechnung extension in UBL (no validator scenario; use the negative_invoice strategy).
- `pdf.paper` and the VIES verification (`validation.drivers.vies.enabled`) take effect; file names of XML-only formats no longer contain a raw translation key.
- Watermarks (draft and preview PDFs) were never printed: mPDF needs `showWatermarkText`, and PDF/A forbids their transparency. Watermarked PDFs without embedded XML are now rendered without PDF/A.
